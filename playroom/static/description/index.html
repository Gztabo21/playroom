Playroom

It is a module created for the menu and the control of the rent of your video game console,

  • You can add your customers.
  • You will store the games
  • You can also enter the types of consoles.
  • You’ll be able to create a ticket for as long as you can use the console and games.
  • It also generates a report with the data of the client, the games, the console and the time of its state.
